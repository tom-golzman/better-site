Privacy Policy

better.io · Effective 17 November 2026

better.io is a personal iPhone app built and used by a single person (the "owner"). It is not offered to the public, has no accounts, and runs no servers. This policy explains what data better.io accesses and how it is handled.

Data better.io reads

  • Apple Health (read-only): active and resting energy, steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight and body fat.
  • Google Health API (read-only), with the owner's permission: activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements recorded by the owner's Fitbit device.
  • Information entered in the app: goal, age, sex, height, weight, foods eaten, recipes and workouts.
  • Public food databases (USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts) and public recipe pages are queried to look up nutrition information. These requests contain search terms or barcodes, not personal data.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the app's features to the owner: calculating calorie and macro targets, logging food and workouts, and showing activity, sleep and recovery trends.

Where data is stored

All data is stored on the owner's iPhone. Google sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone's Keychain. better.io has no server and does not upload health data anywhere. If iCloud sync is enabled in a future version, data would sync only to the owner's own iCloud account.

Sharing

better.io does not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties, does not use data for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. better.io never writes data to Apple Health or Google Health.

Google API Services

better.io's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

Access to Google data can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Apple Health access can be changed in the iPhone's Settings › Health › Data Access. Deleting the app deletes all data it stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email listed on better.io's Google sign-in consent screen.
