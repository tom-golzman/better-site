Better

A personal trainer and nutritionist app for iPhone, built for one person's own use.

What it does

Better sets calorie and macro targets from a fitness goal, logs food and gym workouts, and shows activity, sleep and recovery data from Apple Health and Google Health (Fitbit) so the day's next step is always clear.

Who it's for

Better is a private, single-user app. It is not distributed on the App Store and does not offer accounts or sign-ups.

Privacy

Health and fitness data stays on the owner's iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
